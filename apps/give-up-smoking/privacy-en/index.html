Scope and the key distinction

This notice covers local records, purchase services, notifications and file sharing you choose in Give Up Smoking. Opening the app, completing a form or reading this notice does not by itself constitute explicit consent to all processing or transfers. Operating-system permissions and explicit consent are separate.

Records created on your device

Local records include country/language, quit date, daily cigarette amount, pack size/price, cigarette duration, smoking entries, earlier attempts, selected reasons/triggers, support preferences, support feedback and optional text/photo. They are used for timers, estimated savings, history, summaries of your records and reminders you choose. Avoid unnecessary identifying, contact, health or third-party information in free text. An app account is not required.

Local storage and security

The current app code has no function uploading these quit records to a publisher server or passing them as parameters to purchase services. This does not mean the app makes no network connections. On mobile, the primary record file is AES-GCM encrypted and its key is held in operating-system secure storage. In the web preview, records and the key are in the same browser environment; this is not equivalent to mobile secure storage. Encryption cannot eliminate risks from an unlocked/compromised device, shared files or browser access.

Advertising service

This version has no ads in either free or Premium use. Google AdMob, Google UMP and the advertising integration have been removed. Device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics are not sent to those advertising services. The app has no ad-consent or ad-privacy form.

Purchases and subscriptions

On iPhone, purchases, subscriptions and purchase restoration use Apple StoreKit. Access is derived from Apple-signed transactions verified on-device; the iPhone app does not connect to the RevenueCat purchase service. Apple may process purchase, account and connection information for store operations under its own terms. On Android, Google Play handles payment and the existing RevenueCat integration remains. On Android, RevenueCat may process purchase history, product/transaction and entitlement data, and a generated app user identifier for validation, access and purchase analytics. App code does not set RevenueCat customer attributes containing names, emails, photos, quit reasons or smoking records. The app does not ask for card numbers or bank passwords. Store accounts and payment data are subject to provider terms. The web preview does not initialize the RevenueCat purchase SDK and the test preview takes no payment.

Notifications, photos and external links

Notifications are scheduled on-device; app code does not register a push token with a remote notification server. Notifications use general text by default. If you separately enable the preference under Settings > Notifications > Notification privacy, written and selected quit reasons may be included and visible to others on the lock screen. This is separate from notification permission. Disabling the preference rebuilds pending app notifications with general text; previously delivered notifications can be removed separately from the phone’s notification centre. This personal-text preference is not included in backups. You can disable them in app/device settings. A photo is obtained only when you select one and is processed on-device. Opening a source/provider link connects your browser to that site, which may process IP addresses and standard connection data under its policy. Mobile and web-preview records are separate; the web host may process ordinary access/security logs under its service terms.

PDFs, celebration images and backup files

PDF reports are generated on-device; the base summary and optional fields are shown in the preview. Free text/photos may identify you. PDFs are not password-encrypted. A manual backup is encrypted with your chosen password. The recommended default is a hard-to-guess password of at least 12 characters; the optional six-digit choice has limited strength and someone who obtains the file can attempt passwords offline. Encryption is not a guarantee against cracking. Older backups open with their original passwords. You initiate file saving/sharing. This can transfer the file to your selected app, recipient or cloud service under their retention/sharing terms. Temporary exports stay in a dedicated app cache directory and are cleaned by the on-device deletion action. The document-picker cache copy made when importing a backup is cleaned after reading; its external source is not deleted. The app does not guarantee immediate cleanup after every share or forensic erasure.

Celebration card sharing

A celebration card for an earned achievement is created on your device. It displays smoke-free time, estimated savings, estimated cigarettes avoided and estimated time saved together. Share my achievement prepares a PNG containing these four values and the app logo; the image is not password-encrypted. Sharing is optional. Check the card, sharing app and recipient before sending. Completing the action can transfer the image to your chosen app, recipient or cloud service, which may retain, copy or reshare it under its own terms. The publisher cannot delete these external copies from within the app. Creating the card does not upload quit records to a publisher server. Local viewing information helps avoid repeated celebrations; on mobile this preference is held in operating-system secure storage.

New version checks

On mobile, the app may check in the background for a newer published version when you reach the home screen. On iPhone, the default request goes to Apple’s public store service with the public app identifier and your selected country code. If the publisher configures a different HTTPS release-information address, that address is used; Android checks require such an address. The service may receive your IP address and standard network-request information. App code does not add quit dates, smoking records, savings, reasons, photos or payment details to this request. Choosing Later stores the selected version number as a local device preference. Update opens the official store page; this notice does not itself install a new version or require an update. A missing connection or failed check does not block normal use. TestFlight-only builds are not listed by the default App Store lookup.

Purposes, legal bases and international processing

Personal quit and support records may reveal health information. Separate explicit consent is requested under Article 6(3)(a) of Türkiye’s Law No. 6698 for processing these records on-device to provide timers, history, personal support summaries and reports. Consent is separate from the notice and terms and is not preselected. Explicit consent is not assumed. Without consenting, you can use general support and country support links that need no personal records. Personal tracking does not start before consent. Withdraw under Settings > About > Personal record choices: the confirmed withdrawal/deletion stops personal tracking, clears app reminders and deletes the relevant local records. Withdrawal does not retrospectively make earlier processing unlawful. Your choice, date, language and notice version stay on this device and do not transfer through backups. Purchase validation supports performance of the service contract; support and disputes may involve applicable legal obligations or establishing, exercising or defending claims. Apple, Google Play and, on Android, RevenueCat may process data abroad for their own services. These operations are separate from local quit records, which the app does not send to these providers. Your chosen sharing, email and external links also connect to the relevant services. Local tracking consent is not blanket consent to international transfers.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Primary local records remain until you delete them or the device/browser removes them; the publisher cannot remotely access them to set retention. Settings > About > Delete data on my device removes primary app records, app-owned temporary PDF/PNG/backup files and backup-picker cache copies, and attempts to clear app reminders. Cleanup failure is reported rather than confirmed as complete. It does not cancel subscriptions or delete copies in Files, cloud storage, store/provider records or recipients’ copies. Retention and deletion of service records held by Apple, Google Play and, on Android, RevenueCat follow each provider’s policy and legal obligations. The on-device deletion button cannot remove those providers’ records. Applicable law may provide information, access, rectification, erasure, recipient-notification, objection, consent-withdrawal and compensation rights. Use the contact channel above without sending unnecessary health records or identity documents. Local records unavailable to the publisher are edited/deleted on your device. Requests are handled under applicable procedures/deadlines; regulatory complaints and judicial remedies remain available.

Children and notice changes

Give Up Smoking is intended for adults and is not directed to children. The app does not verify age through identity documents or another method. A store age rating is not in-app age verification. Changes to processing purposes/providers require an updated notice and, where needed, separate information and permission/consent mechanisms. New legal notices are provided in Turkish and English; other app languages display English.
